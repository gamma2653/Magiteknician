A spell can be seen to land. It crosses to the foe as a streak in the colours of its runes, bursts where it gets through, and flares on a ward that stops it. The grade of a cast is written beside its caster. A ward is a ring round its caster that runs down with the ward's time, and a chill is icicles round the rim. How much a spell did is how big it is drawn.
