The opponents speak. Each greets you before the duel, remarks on it as it goes, and has the last word when it is over. The campaign tells what happens before each duel and after it, won or lost, and has an epilogue. The card over the arena can be read: it no longer lets the runes under it show through, and it stays in the middle of the screen however much is on it.
