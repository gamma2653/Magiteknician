The workshop, where a spell is made of runes. What it does follows from the runes it is made of, and what it costs from what it does and how hard it is to cast. Eight can be kept. They are in the practice range, and in the campaign a spell made of runes you have learned can be brought to a duel. A recording of a duel has the spells that were made for it.
