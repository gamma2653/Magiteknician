The keys can be chosen in the options: the seven runes, hearing the spell and giving up a cast. A key that is in use changes places with the one it replaces. The runes can be had in seven colours that can be told apart by an eye that does not see red, or green, or blue. As they were painted, two of them are all but one colour to an eye that does not see red.
